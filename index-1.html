Terms and conditions

Please read these before paying. Edit the wording to match your business.

1. What you are paying for

The KES 800 fee gives you access to view the list of available units for 3 days. It is a viewing fee and does not reserve any unit or pay any rent or deposit.

2. Access code

Your code is tied to the phone number used to pay and cannot be transferred. Access ends 3 days after payment.

3. Refunds

The fee is non-refundable once your code has been issued, except where a payment was taken but no code was delivered. Contact us within 24 hours in that case.

4. Availability

Units are updated regularly, but a unit may be taken before you view it. We do not guarantee that any unit will still be available.

5. Viewings and agreements

Any rent, deposit, or tenancy agreement is made directly between you and the landlord. Never pay rent or deposits before viewing a unit and signing an agreement.

6. Acceptable use

Do not copy, resell, or republish our listings, photos, or access codes. We may cancel access for misuse without refund.

7. Your data

We use your phone number only to process payment, issue your code, and send you notifications. We do not sell it.
